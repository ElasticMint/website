Contact

Get in touch

Book a 30-minute call or send us an email. The first conversation is free, and there’s no obligation on either side.

Book a Meeting Send an Email

We’re a remote team working with clients across the UK and internationally, offering bespoke software development, technology consultancy and fractional CTO support.

Whether you have a defined project in mind, a problem you’re not yet sure how to solve, or you simply want to find out whether Elastic Mint might be a good fit, we welcome the conversation. We’ll listen to what you’re trying to do, ask questions to understand the constraints, and only suggest a way forward if we’re confident we can help. There’s no hard sell, and we’re happy to sign an NDA if you want to discuss anything sensitive.

The earlier we’re brought into the conversation, the more useful we tend to be. We aim to respond to all enquiries within two working days.

Based in Bristol, UK
